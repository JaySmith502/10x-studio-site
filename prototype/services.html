// What we build

Five things we do well.

Custom web platforms, ecommerce, internal tools, AI workflows and integrations — engineered as one system, not five disconnected line items. Pick the one closest to your problem; we usually end up building two of these together.

01 · Web Platforms

Custom Web Platforms

For businesses that need more than a brochure site — companies whose website is supposed to capture leads, route requests, gate access, or run a multi-location operation.

Custom pages & layouts
User accounts & role-based access
Admin dashboards
Forms & intake flows
CRM & payment integrations
Notifications & automations
Outcome A platform that reflects how the business actually operates — not a template padded out with marketing copy.
02 · Ecommerce

Ecommerce & Product Platforms

For brands that need to sell online with more sophistication than a basic storefront — configurable products, multi-location inventory, subscription billing, custom checkout.

Product catalogs & configurators
Cart & checkout experiences
Customer accounts & order history
Subscription & membership
Payment & shipping integrations
Franchise / location-aware logic
Outcome Online selling that does not make customers (or your fulfillment team) want to throw a laptop into a river.
03 · Internal Tools

Internal Business Tools

For teams whose business is currently held together by spreadsheets, shared inboxes, and a Slack channel where people copy-paste data between four apps.

Admin dashboards
Approval workflows
Job & inventory tracking
Reporting & analytics
Vendor / client management
Document intake & routing
Outcome The system the business has been trying to fake with spreadsheets — finally built.
04 · AI Workflows

AI-Enabled Workflow Tools

For companies that want practical AI embedded into operations — not a chatbot bolted onto a homepage.

AI customer support assistants
Intake triage & routing
Document summarization
RAG over company knowledge
Lead qualification
Internal decision support
Outcome AI that reduces friction, speeds work, and earns its keep — with guardrails an enterprise security team would actually approve.
05 · Integrations

Integrations & Automation

For companies whose software tools refuse to talk to each other — CRM, forms, payments, notifications, reporting, all living in disconnected silos.

CRM integrations (HubSpot, Salesforce)
API connections & data sync
n8n / Make / Zapier workflows
Form-to-database automation
Email / SMS notification systems
Reporting pipelines
Outcome Your stack stops behaving like a group project. Tools share data, work routes itself, leadership sees the truth in one place.
