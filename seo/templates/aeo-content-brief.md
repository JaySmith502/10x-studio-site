Pick at least one the top-5 results don't have:

---

## 4. Brand-voice & GEO guardrails (check while writing)

- [ ] **Declarative, not hedged** — no "we think", "might", "could", "possibly".
- [ ] **Not overly promotional** — no "unlock your potential", "next-gen",
      "light-years ahead". Specific beats breathless.
- [ ] **Complete** — prices, specs, and comparisons stated, not implied.
- [ ] **Sectioned & scannable** — no wall-of-text; one idea per section.
- [ ] **Cited** — one number, source, or concrete example per section.
- [ ] **Fresh** — `datePublished` set; revisit `dateModified` on update.
- [ ] Address the client as "you", speak as "we" (one accountable team).
